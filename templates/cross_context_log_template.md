# Cross‑Context Log Template

Record significant actions or updates that should be shared across agents and platforms. Use one entry per event.

- **Date & Time:** (YYYY‑MM‑DD HH:MM, Europe/Sarajevo)
- **Agent/Platform:** (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot)
- **Context:** Briefly describe what was done or discovered.
- **Impact:** Explain how this affects ongoing projects or workflows.
- **Next Steps:** List any follow‑up actions required.
- **Related Docs/Prompts:** Link to any relevant files or prompt packs.
- **Notes:** Additional comments or observations.

*Example:*

- Date & Time: 2025‑09‑18 10:30
- Agent/Platform: ChatGPT
- Context: Completed deep research sprint on AI SaaS tools; produced executive brief.
- Impact: Identified three new monetization opportunities for Brzi Ai channel.
- Next Steps: Prepare scripts to pitch offers #2 and #3; update monetization strategy document.
- Related Docs/Prompts: `/prompts/deep_research_sprint.md`
- Notes: Need to verify financial viability of tool #2 before proceeding.
