# Music Prompt Template

Craft a clear and compelling prompt for Producer or Suno to generate music in the style you desire. Include genre, mood, instrumentation, and language cues.

- **Genre / Style:** (e.g., glitch techno, cyberpunk rap, Balkan fusion)
- **Mood / Atmosphere:** (e.g., dark, uplifting, futuristic)
- **Tempo:** (e.g., BPM range or general feel)
- **Language(s) for Lyrics:** (e.g., English, Bosnian, Japanese)
- **Instruments / Sounds:** (e.g., synth bass, distorted guitars, ethnic percussion)
- **Reference Tracks or Artists:** (optional)
- **Narrative / Theme:** (optional – describe the story or imagery you want the music to evoke)
- **Other Notes:** (e.g., include rap verses, female vocals, extended intro/outro)
