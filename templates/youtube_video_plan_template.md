## Structure / Beat Sheet

1. **Intro:** Introduce yourself and the topic (approx. 15‑30 seconds).
2. **Context:** Explain why this topic matters.
3. **Main Points:** List 3‑5 key points or steps; include timestamp cues.
4. **Demo / Examples:** Describe any demos, screen recordings, or live code.
5. **Conclusion:** Summarize the key takeaway and what viewers should do next.
