# Outreach Script Template

Use this template to craft compelling outreach messages. Customize details for your audience and medium (DM, email, video).

## Introduction

- Briefly introduce yourself and establish credibility (e.g., “Hi [Name], I’m Arnes from Brzi Ai…”).

## Pain Point

- State the problem you believe the recipient is facing. Show empathy and understanding.

## Promise & Solution

- Explain how your offer addresses the pain point. Highlight benefits rather than features.

## Proof

- Provide evidence that your solution works (e.g., testimonials, results, case studies, personal experience).

## Call to Action

- Clearly state the next step you want them to take (e.g., schedule a call, reply to the email, click a link). Keep it simple.

## Closing

- Keep it polite and open‑ended. Offer to answer any questions. Include contact information or relevant links.
