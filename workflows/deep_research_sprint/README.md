# Deep Research Sprint Workflow

## Purpose

Conduct a focused research sprint that yields a concise executive brief, an opportunity map, monetization strategies, sourced citations, and next actions. This workflow is designed to produce maximum insight in minimal time.

## Steps

1. **Define the Topic:** Clearly state the research question or area of investigation. Include any relevant context or constraints.
2. **Prepare Tools:** Select the appropriate agent (e.g., ChatGPT with web search connectors) and gather any initial sources or keywords.
3. **Search & Summarize:** Perform web searches and gather information. Use a mix of broad and targeted queries. Summarize key findings in bullet points, capturing statistics, trends, and notable insights.
4. **Create the Executive Brief (10 sentences):** Write a concise narrative that summarizes the most important findings, including context, challenges, and opportunities.
5. **Build the Opportunity Map:** Identify potential opportunities (quick wins) versus effort levels. Organize them in a 2×2 matrix or simple table: high vs. low impact and effort.
6. **Develop Monetization Plays:** Propose three possible monetization strategies. For each, outline cost, setup time, risk, and provide an example or reference.
7. **Compile Sources:** List all sources used, including a one‑line explanation of their relevance. Ensure each fact in your brief is traceable.
8. **Generate Next Actions:** Recommend five concrete next steps. For each, specify the owner (You vs. AI), estimated impact, and required effort. Flag quick wins.
9. **Review & Iterate:** Check for completeness, clarity, and alignment with the mission. Iterate as needed based on feedback.
10. **Log Findings:** Record key findings and decisions in the relevant `logs/` file and update any shared context documents if necessary.

## Deliverables

- **Executive Brief (10 sentences)**
- **Opportunity Map (table or matrix)**
- **3 Monetization Plays** with cost, setup time, risk, and example
- **List of Sources** with one‑line relevance notes
- **5 Next Actions** with impact/effort and owner designation

Use the prompt in `/prompts/deep_research_sprint.md` to initiate this workflow with an AI agent.
