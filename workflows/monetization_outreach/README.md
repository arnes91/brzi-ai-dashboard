# Monetization & Outreach Workflow

## Purpose

Identify low‑risk revenue opportunities and execute outreach to potential clients, partners, or audiences. This workflow helps you generate offers, craft messages, set up a CRM, and plan a 14‑day outreach cadence.

## Steps

1. **Opportunity Ideation:** Brainstorm at least five potential offers (products, services, partnerships). For each, define the problem it solves, the promise, proof (case studies or results), price range, and platform (e.g., website, YouTube, direct email).
2. **Prioritization:** Rank the offers by ease of implementation, potential revenue, and alignment with the Brzi brand. Select the top three to pursue first.
3. **Script Writing:** Create outreach scripts for direct messages (DMs), emails, and short videos. Include personalized hooks, pain points, and clear calls to action. Use `/templates/outreach_script_template.md` for guidance.
4. **CRM Setup:** Design a simple spreadsheet or use a CRM tool to track leads. Fields should include status (e.g., contacted, interested, closed), last contact date, potential value, and next steps.
5. **14‑Day Cadence:** Plan daily micro‑tasks that take 30 minutes or less. Tasks might include sending DMs, following up on emails, publishing a promotional post, or updating the CRM.
6. **Execute Outreach:** Start contacting leads based on your scripts. Personalize each message and document responses in the CRM.
7. **Track Progress:** Monitor response rates, conversions, and feedback. Adjust scripts or offers based on performance.
8. **Follow Up:** Schedule follow‑ups at appropriate intervals. Use a tool to remind you when it’s time to reach out again.
9. **Review & Adjust:** At the end of 14 days, review results. Identify which offers performed best and refine future outreach plans.
10. **Log Results:** Record metrics and insights in the `logs/` directory. Update the cross‑context document if there are important strategic shifts.

## Deliverables

- **List of 5+ Offers** (pain, promise, proof, price, platform)
- **3 Outreach Scripts** (DM/email/video)
- **CRM Schema** (spreadsheet or tool setup)
- **14‑Day Outreach Plan**
- **Metrics Report** (optional summary after execution)

Use the prompt in `/prompts/monetization_outreach.md` to initiate this workflow with an AI agent.
