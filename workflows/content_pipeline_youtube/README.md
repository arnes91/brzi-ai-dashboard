# Content Pipeline – YouTube Workflow

## Purpose

Design a streamlined process for producing a single YouTube video from concept to upload. This workflow ensures that each step—from ideation through scripting, editing, and publishing—is efficient and consistent with brand guidelines.

## Steps

1. **Idea Generation:** Brainstorm video topics aligned with your channels (Brzi Ai or Brzi Balkan). Use analytics, trending topics, or viewer feedback to choose a theme.
2. **Create Video Brief:** Define the title, hook, promise, structure, key talking points, b‑roll ideas, and call to action. Use the template in `/templates/youtube_video_plan_template.md`.
3. **Write Script:** Develop a beat sheet or storyboard. Write on‑screen text or narration. Keep the tone engaging and concise. Include prompts for cutaways or b‑roll.
4. **Plan SEO:** Draft an SEO package with A/B tested titles, 10 keywords/tags, a short description (~100 characters), and thumbnail text suggestions.
5. **Asset Gathering:** Collect or produce necessary assets—music tracks, sound effects, images, and stock footage. Document sources for licensing.
6. **Editing:** Use CapCut or Pippit to assemble the video, following the script. Incorporate b‑roll and transitions. Ensure audio levels and pacing are consistent.
7. **Review & Revise:** Watch the draft and make cuts to improve flow. Check for brand consistency, subtitle accuracy, and compliance with platform guidelines.
8. **Upload Checklist:** Prepare the final files and upload to YouTube. Fill in the title, description, tags, chapters, end screen, cards, playlist selection, and scheduling information.
9. **Cross‑Post & Promote:** Schedule posts to social media. Include teasers, behind‑the‑scenes clips, or highlights to drive traffic.
10. **Analytics & Feedback:** Monitor performance through YouTube Analytics. Note watch time, retention curves, and comments. Adjust future content accordingly.

## Deliverables

- **Video Brief**
- **Script / Beat Sheet**
- **SEO Pack** (title options, tags, short description, thumbnail text)
- **Editing Plan** with asset list and b‑roll cues
- **Upload Checklist** with post‑publication tasks

Use the prompt in `/prompts/content_pipeline_youtube.md` to initiate this workflow with an AI agent.
