# Prompt Pack – Content Pipeline (YouTube)

Use this prompt to guide an AI agent through the creation of a YouTube video from concept to upload.

## Goal

Design and produce a high‑quality video that aligns with the Brzi Ai or Brzi Balkan channel, within a total production time of four hours or less.

## Deliverables

- **Video Brief:** Title options, hook, promise, structure, b‑roll cues.
- **Script / Beat Sheet:** Outline of narration and visuals.
- **SEO Pack:** Two title options, 10 tags, 100‑character description, thumbnail text.
- **Editing Plan:** List of assets, b‑roll cues, and editing notes.
- **Upload Checklist:** Steps to publish the video and optimize post‑production settings.

## Instructions

1. Confirm the video’s topic and intended audience.
2. Brainstorm a compelling hook and structure the narrative with clear sections.
3. Generate a beat sheet covering introduction, context, key points, examples, and conclusion.
4. Suggest relevant b‑roll and audio cues.
5. Craft two SEO‑optimized title options and associated metadata.
6. Outline editing steps and identify necessary assets.
7. Prepare a checklist for uploading, including cards, end screens, playlists, and cross‑posting.
8. Present all deliverables in a clear, organized format.

This prompt assumes the Base Context has been provided. Modify the topic and channel context before issuing the prompt.
