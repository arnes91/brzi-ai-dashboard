# Prompt Pack – Deep Research Sprint

Use this prompt to instruct an AI agent to conduct a focused research sprint.

## Goal

Produce a concise, monetization‑oriented brief with sources. Summarize the current landscape, opportunities, risks, and propose a low‑effort, high‑impact path.

## Scope

- Research the specified topic thoroughly.
- Rank information by trustworthiness and recency.
- Extract factual insights; avoid speculation.

## Deliverables

- **10‑Sentence Executive Brief** summarizing context, challenges, opportunities, and recommendations.
- **Opportunity Map** categorizing opportunities by impact and effort.
- **3 Monetization Plays** with cost, setup time, risk, and examples.
- **Sources** list with one‑line relevance notes.
- **5 Next Actions** with owner, impact, and effort (flag quick wins).

## Instructions

1. Ask clarifying questions only if absolutely necessary.
2. Structure the executive brief clearly; each sentence should convey one idea.
3. When mapping opportunities, distinguish between high vs. low impact and effort.
4. Cite all sources properly and double‑check facts.
5. Provide actionable next steps.

This prompt assumes the Base Context has been provided. Adjust the topic-specific details before issuing the prompt.
