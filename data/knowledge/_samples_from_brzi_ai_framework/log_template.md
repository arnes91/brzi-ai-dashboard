# General Log Entry Template

Use this template to record any event, meeting, or task that needs tracking.

- **Date & Time:** (YYYY‑MM‑DD HH:MM, Europe/Sarajevo)
- **Title:** (Short description of the log entry)
- **Participants/Agents:** (Who was involved or what agent performed the task?)
- **Description:** Describe what happened, why it matters, and any decisions made.
- **Outcome:** What was achieved or produced?
- **Action Items:** List any follow‑up tasks and assign owners.
- **Attachments/Links:** Provide links to relevant documents, code, or resources.
- **Additional Notes:** Add any observations or thoughts for future reference.
