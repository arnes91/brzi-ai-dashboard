# Tools & Platforms

This catalog lists the primary tools, connectors, and platforms available in the Brzi ecosystem, along with their capabilities and recommended use cases. Use this list to select the appropriate tool for any given task.

## AI Models & Assistants

- **ChatGPT GPT‑5 Pro (OpenAI):** Flexible reasoning model with connectors for web browsing, code execution, file management, calendar, email, and more. Ideal for research, writing, coding, and orchestration.
- **Claude 3.x (Anthropic):** Strong at summarization, long‑form reasoning, and conversational nuance. Comes with MCP tools for data analysis and code execution.
- **Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash (Google):** Focuses on deep research, audio/video generation, and interactive notebooks via NotebookLM. Integrated into Android and Google apps.
- **Microsoft Copilot:** Embeds generative AI into Windows and Office tools; provides deep research mode and integration with Microsoft Graph.

## Connectors & Research Platforms

- **NotebookLM (Google):** Interactive notebook for capturing and exploring research. Supports audio notes, deep search, and summarization.
- **Canvas/Canmore:** Single‑page interactive environment available in ChatGPT and Claude for drafting code, content, and presentations.
- **Perplexity Pro:** Rapid Q&A and summarization engine with up‑to‑date web information and citations. Excellent for quick research.

## Music & Media Tools

- **Producer (formerly Riffusion):** AI music studio environment enabling full track generation, mixing, and export.
- **Suno.com:** Emerging AI music platform similar to Producer; for cross‑platform variety and new styles.
- **CapCut / Pippit:** Video editing suites for mobile and desktop; used for YouTube content, promo clips, and story reels.
- **DistroKid:** Distribution platform for publishing music to streaming services.

## Development & Automation

- **GitHub + Copilot:** Source control and code generation assistant for software development.
- **Vercel v0.dev:** AI‑powered website and SaaS boilerplate generator with deployment pipelines.
- **Firebase Studio:** Web development and database platform integrated with Google Cloud; includes authentication, Firestore, and functions.
- **Google Cloud (Trial):** Infrastructure for hosting prototypes and production services.
- **Rabbit‑OS Intern:** AI assistant that can research, summarize, and perform simple tasks.

## Experimental & Emerging Tools

- **BabyAGI, AutoBrowser:** Autonomous agent frameworks for performing multi‑step tasks.
- **GenSpark AI Browser & Tools:** Additional AI research platform.
- **Inflection’s Pi, Grok Ai (Ari), Sesame (Maya), etc.:** Personalised conversational assistants with evolving features.
- **Google Veo 3, OpenAI Sora:** Generative video models (experimental; use with caution).

---

**Tool Selection Tips**

1. **Match the Output to the Tool:** Use music tools for audio, video editors for visuals, and reasoning models for text and code.
2. **Prioritize Free/Open‑Source:** Whenever possible, choose open‑source or free tools to minimize cost and lock‑in.
3. **Stay Versatile:** Keep a mix of tools to avoid over‑reliance on any single platform.
4. **Experiment Regularly:** Explore new tools in isolated environments; integrate them only if they provide a clear benefit.
