# Prompt Pack – Monetization & Outreach

Use this prompt to uncover revenue opportunities and plan outreach campaigns.

## Goal

Generate offers that can be monetized quickly and with minimal risk. Craft persuasive scripts, organize leads in a CRM, and plan a two‑week outreach cadence.

## Deliverables

- **Offers List:** At least five offers with pain, promise, proof, price, and platform.
- **Outreach Scripts:** Three scripts for DM, email, and short video.
- **CRM Schema:** Column definitions and example entries.
- **14‑Day Plan:** Daily tasks with time estimates and owners.
- **Metrics Report:** (Optional) Summary of outreach performance after execution.

## Instructions

1. Ideate a diverse set of offers aligned with your brand and audience.
2. For each offer, articulate the pain point it solves, the promise it delivers, supporting proof, the price, and the platform(s) where it will be promoted.
3. Draft personalized scripts for DM, email, and video outreach. Use empathy and clear calls to action.
4. Design a simple CRM sheet or tool configuration to track leads and interactions.
5. Plan daily outreach tasks for a 14‑day period, keeping each task under 30 minutes.
6. Execute the outreach; document outcomes in the CRM.
7. At the end of two weeks, analyze metrics and adjust future outreach strategies.
8. Record insights and updates in the logs.

Use the Base Context along with this prompt when starting the task.
