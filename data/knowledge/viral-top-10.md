- Add small background details for rewatch value
- Include easter eggs for tech-savvy viewers
- Maintain high contrast and vibrant colors
- Time transitions to potential music beats
- End each with a small twist or callback

- Quick cuts for timing/comedy

- Perfect loop potential for all clips
